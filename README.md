## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm a final-year B.Tech student in AI & ML, and somewhere along the way of building ML projects, I got completely hooked on what happens *before* the model — the pipelines, the warehouses, the architecture that makes everything actually work.

That curiosity pushed me deep into **Data Engineering and MLOps**, where I've been building end-to-end systems: from raw data ingestion through Snowflake-based warehouses, to ML pipelines with CI/CD deployment on Docker and GitHub Actions.

- 🔭 Currently focused on **Data Engineering**, **MLOps**, and **scalable analytics systems**
- 🌱 Exploring **Databricks**, **Apache Spark**, and advanced **data pipeline architecture**
- 🏫 Mentored 30+ students at **Skoolam NGO** — teaching problem-solving, not just syntax
- 📚 Outside tech: I read Hindi poetry and novels — Dinkar, Premchand, Dharamveer Bharti
- 🎯 700+ problems solved across LeetCode, GFG, Codeforces, CodeChef
- 📍 Bhopal, India | Open to opportunities

> *"Data without pipelines is just noise. I build the plumbing that makes the signal matter."*
